The Real Science of Habit Formation
Why 21 days is a myth, how the habit loop actually works, and the exact system that makes new behaviours stick for life.
Key Insight: Research by Phillippa Lally (2010) found it takes an average of 66 days — not 21 — for a behaviour to become automatic. The range was 18–254 days depending on complexity and individual differences. GlowAI accounts for this by gradually increasing habit complexity over your 90-day plan, rather than dumping everything at once.

Fitness 12 min read
Progressive Overload: The Only Rule That Matters
The single most important principle in strength training — and exactly how to apply it without getting injured or hitting plateaus.
Key Insight: Progressive overload means consistently increasing the demand placed on your musculoskeletal system. This can be achieved by increasing weight, reps, sets, frequency, or reducing rest periods. A systematic 2.5–5% weight increase every 2 weeks is optimal for intermediate lifters. Your GlowAI workout plan implements this automatically.

Sleep 10 min read
Sleep, Cortisol, and Body Composition
How sleep deprivation directly sabotages fat loss, muscle retention, and willpower — and what the research says you should do about it.
Key Insight: A 2010 study in the Annals of Internal Medicine found that sleep-deprived subjects lost 55% less fat and 60% more lean mass than those who slept 8.5 hours, despite identical calorie deficits. Cortisol (stress hormone) rises with sleep deprivation, directly causing muscle catabolism and fat retention, especially around the abdomen.

Nutrition 9 min read
How Much Protein Do You Actually Need?
The latest research cuts through the noise on protein intake, timing, and what actually drives muscle growth and fat loss.
Key Insight: Current meta-analyses suggest 1.6–2.2g protein per kg of bodyweight for muscle building (Morton et al., 2018). Protein timing matters less than total daily intake — the "anabolic window" myth has largely been debunked. What matters most: hitting your daily target, spread across 3–5 meals.
